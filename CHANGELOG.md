# Changelog

## Unreleased

- November 6, 2026: the repository is created. The draft v0 specification moves here from Sau's `docs/NULL.md`. The name Null and the `.noll` extension were chosen by the user.
- November 7, 2026: section 6 says a value with a unit is converted exactly and rounded once, and that NaN is written `nan` without its sign or payload, the one value that does not round-trip; section 4's bevel example is written `{ width 4mm; segments 3 }`, two attributes (Sau's decision S59).
- November 7, 2026: section 8 lists Null's forms over RNA for the slice (project, units, members, `data`, material slots, primitives, `vertices` and `polygons`, node trees, light power, lens millimetres) and that defaults are a new datablock's; section 18 lists every diagnostic code; section 6 defines the sRGB rule. Section 4's examples follow the schema: a primitive cube's `size` is one length, scaled by its object, and `layout` has one node per `;`. Task 1a-06.
- November 7, 2026: section 15 fixes the formatter's layout and section 17 the per-value rules of the smallest write-back edit; a value printed in a unit keeps that unit whenever a decimal in it reads back (task 1a-07).
- November 7, 2026: a primitive's proportions come from its add operator's `scale`, not its object's (section 8): the pier example is `primitive cube size=1m scale=[1.2 3 0.08]` with object scale `[1 1 1]`, so its 4 mm bevel stays 4 mm. Replaces the rule of task 1a-06 that a box of other proportions is a cube scaled by its object. Task 1a-06b, after Sau's 1a-05c.
- November 7, 2026: section 6 lists every RNA unit (`PROP_UNIT_*`) with what a bare number means, the words Null accepts with their exact values, and how the scene's unit scale and frame rate enter, following Blender's own rule for scaling (task 1c-01a). New words beyond the slice's: area and volume, mass, frames as seconds, absolute time, velocity, acceleration, camera units besides `mm`, power prefixes, temperature, wavelength, colour temperature and frequency; implementing them is task 1c-01b.
- November 7, 2026: the checker reads seconds on a float frame property through its scene's frame rate, written on the scene as `fps` and `fps-base` (a new form, section 8); an integer frame stays bare, and seconds outside a scene are an error. Write-back keeps seconds (`null_print::write_in`). Task 1c-01c.
- November 7, 2026: the checker and write-back divide a value written with a unit by the scene's unit scale, as section 6 says, and section 6 says which scale: a scene's own `units` inside its block, the project's outside any scene (or the one scene's that sets any), never with the system `none`. Task 1c-01d.
- November 7, 2026: every word of section 6's units table is read, converted exactly with one rounding, and printed back in the word its line uses: area, volume, mass, seconds, velocity, acceleration, the camera's `cm`, `m` and `um`, power's prefixes, temperature with its offsets, wavelength, colour temperature and frequency, besides the slice's lengths, angles, lens and watts. Task 1c-01b.
- November 7, 2026: section 6 gives the written form of every RNA type (booleans, integers, floats, enums, flag sets, strings, pointers, collections, arrays, multi-dimensional arrays, matrices): matrices row by row as `mathutils` prints them, a string that is not valid UTF-8 as the list of its byte values (Sau's decision S65, revising S61's `\xNN`, which Scowr's lexer refuses), an empty pointer `none`, a too-long string an error (S61 (5)). Task 1c-02a.
- November 7, 2026: section 6 says Blender's float32 unit factors make values typed into Blender's fields differ from Null's exact rule for many values, not only °F (526 of 2,000 in `cm`; `9cm` is 0.089999996 there and 0.090000004 in Null), that Null's rule stands, and that Sau's fields follow it (Sau's decision S64, task 1c-01g); also that Null's list of unit words is its own choice, not Blender's rarely-used marking. Task 1c-01a, after review.
- November 7, 2026: section 7 specifies sub-object handles: made from the name, unique within their owner across kinds, paths with `/` and sockets with `.`, and for each kind of sub-object the Blender field that keeps its identity across a rename (modifiers' `persistent_uid`, nodes' `identifier`, sockets' and group sockets' identifiers, key blocks' `uid`, action slots' `handle`) or, where Blender has only the name, Sau's map `.sau/ids.noll` (Sau's decision S61). `@rig/@spine.002` is now `@rig/@spine-002`, since `.` names a socket or a pattern's copy. Task 1b-02a.
- November 7, 2026: section 7 names the sub-object lines the checker indexes and says it resolves paths against them, with the path written right when one names nothing; a handle two sub-objects of one owner share is a `null-handle` error whatever their kinds. Task 1b-02c.
- November 7, 2026: section 7 says a path written inside its owner's block starts at that owner's own sub-objects first, the owner being one datablock (kind and handle). Task 1b-02c, after review.
- November 7, 2026: section 7 says that once a path's first step is the owner's own sub-object the path stays the owner's, so a later misspelling keeps its fix and lists the owner's sub-objects rather than falling through to a datablock of that handle; section 6 says 29.97 fps may also be Blender's preset `fps 30` with `fps-base 1.001` (a float32, not exactly 1.001), and that an action's keys, outside every scene, take no seconds. Task 1b-02c, second review; 1c-01c's review notes.
- November 7, 2026: section 6 says which scale holds outside every scene when the project has no `units`: the one every scene has, and where they differ a `null-unit` error on the first value written with a scaled unit there; an empty `units {}` is the project's units at a new scene's settings; only the first `units` line and the first of each setting count, the others `null-repeated`. One reader, `null_check::units`, serves the checker, write-back and Sau (task 1c-01d, after review).
- November 7, 2026: section 7 says what the persistent ID is (128 random bits in DNA) and what renaming, saving, undo, copying, duplicating, appending, linking, library overrides and old files do to it, and that the handle map is `.sau/ids.noll` (Sau's decisions S17 and S61). Task 1b-01a; the hooks in Blender are in Sau's ARCHITECTURE.md.
- November 7, 2026: `#rrggbb` reads through a fixed table of the 256 exactly rounded channel values of the IEC 61966-2-1 curve, proven with exact integer arithmetic, so it is the same on every platform; no floating-point `pow` (Sau's decision S62, RFC 0001, task 1c-01f). Blender's colour picker gives other bits for 222 of the 256 values, so a colour from it prints as a list of floats, not `#rrggbb`.
- November 7, 2026: section 7 says an ID is unique within its library and a datablock is identified by its library and ID, that an override keeps its ID through a resync (Sau's decision S64 (2)), and that an old file's derived ID uses the datablock's type as well as its name (S63). Task 1b-01a, after review.
- November 7, 2026: `null_value` reads and prints a value in a scene (`Scene`, `read_in`, `print_in_scene`): a value written with a unit is divided by the unit scale to its dimension's power, as `BKE_unit_value_scale` does, and a frame property's seconds are multiplied by `fps` / `fps-base`, each float taken at its exact value and the whole rounded once. A new dimension, `Frames`, takes the words of seconds. Task 1c-01h.
- November 7, 2026: `schema/null-schema-project.json`, the schema of all project data Sau generates (Sau's task 1b-03b): every struct the datablock types own but the interface's, with every node, modifier, constraint and strip kind, the node properties Blender defines at run time, and enums whose items are computed when used marked `items-at-run-time`. The slice's `schema/null-schema.json` is unchanged; the checker moves to the new file in task 1b-03e. Kept out of the spelling check, since it holds Blender's own names.
- November 7, 2026: the checker runs on the schema of all project data (task 1b-03e): `schema/null-schema-project.json` loads in 3.8 ms (an optimised build; the budget is 50 ms), the slice's project checks clean on it with the same errors as on the slice's schema, and a misspelt property of any datablock type gets its fix. Structs are found through indexes made at load; a two-dimensional array (a matrix, `bound-box`) is written as rows of lists; an enum marked `items-at-run-time` takes values the schema does not list unless they are near one it does. Section 8 names both schema files.
- November 8, 2026: a 4×4 transform matrix's translation column is read and printed as `location` is, through the scene's unit scale, and its other items are bare, so section 6's own example `[[1 0 0 2m] …]` checks (`null_print::print_matrix` writes one from RNA's column-by-column array); section 8 says a value far from every listed item of an `items-at-run-time` enum passes the checker unseen. Task 1b-03e, after review.
- November 8, 2026: `null_value::read_in` returns `ReadError::BadScene`, never panics, for a value whose conversion needs a scene's unit scale or frame rate when that is zero, negative or not finite (`Scene::converts`); `print_in_scene` then prints no unit. A unit scale of 0 divided by zero before, which would abort Sau's typed fields (found reviewing task 1c-01g); a negative, infinite or NaN scale gave a wrong value.
- November 8, 2026: every RNA type is read and printed exactly (`null_print::read_value` and `print_value`, task 1c-02b): booleans, integers in their hard range, floats in their unit through the scene, enum items (free-text ones as strings), flag sets, strings and byte lists, arrays, nested arrays and transform matrices; property tests over the project schema's own properties give back the same bits, integers at their bounds, ±0, subnormals and every flag set included. The checker reads flag sets (another order is an error whose fix is the schema's) and free-text items. Section 6 says a string's length counts its bytes and closing NUL, a 0 byte is an error, and a byte list that is UTF-8 is an error.
- November 8, 2026: canonical order (`null_print::canonical`, task 1c-01e): attributes and `key=value`s in RNA's definition order, base first, Null's forms where the property they write stands, members and node lines in their written order, comments with their lines, no blank lines inside a block, then the formatter's layout; section 15 gives the rules. A shuffled slice file comes back in the one canonical form, and formatting twice changes nothing (property test).
- November 8, 2026: a value outside a property's hard range, an infinity included, is read and written as it is, and the checker's `null-range` for it is a note, not an error (the lead's call on task 1c-02b): a `.blend` can hold one, and Blender clamps it when set. An item of a run-time enum that is not one kebab-case word is written as a string. Task 1c-02b, after review.
- November 8, 2026: after review: an integer past 32 bits is an error, not a range note, and neither read nor written; every item a run-time enum's data gives is written as a string (task 1c-02b). Canonical order keeps a comment after `{` or `;` on its line and gives `units` the spec's short order (task 1c-01e).
